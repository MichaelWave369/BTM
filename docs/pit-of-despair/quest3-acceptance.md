# Physical Quest 3 acceptance — pending

No physical headset was available in this Work session. Automated tracked poses and Chromium software WebGL do not validate the compositor, Touch ergonomics, stereoscopic comfort, device voices, thermals or headset frame rate. An owner/headset tester must perform these checks before treating the candidate as hardware accepted.

- Open the candidate locally over trusted HTTPS or use an owner-approved preview. Test both Classic/Arcade and immersive entry/exit without changing production deployment.
- Start Pit from the in-headset menu, switch to the original campaign and back, then resume each independent checkpoint. Verify the Pit final ending survives browser restart.
- Play First Fall and a complete human Act I with finite ammo. Test dry fire, partial cells, manual X reload, free Slap, left-grip Ori, B Bash and no-target shield under pressure. Assess the supply route, warning readability and whether early difficulty is fair.
- Test all four guardians through all three phases with standing/seated poses. Strafe the ram, escape marked ground, find ring gaps, use each weakness and win with a nearly empty reserve. Check that no projectile requires an uncomfortable head movement.
- Raise and rotate the left wrist during combat. Verify above-hand clearance, COMMS LOG, weapons, map, pinning and neutral rearm. Confirm Ori stays right of the wrist/aim lane and critical floor alerts remain peripheral and readable.
- Check captions off/on, optional device voice and unavailable voice fallback offline. Verify hazard cues are audible without masking attack tells. Record which Quest browser/voice configuration was tested.
- Measure sustained frame timing on device in all quality presets, especially trial 12 rings/marks and dense trial 11. Record dropped frames, draw/vertex counts, thermal conditions and play duration. Do not infer 72 FPS from desktop timings.
- Test offline reload and resume only after the service worker completes an online cache install. Verify original Legacy/Nightmare saves and CODE BROWN/ΦWAD still work; check Gen.E.Wrist explicit travel confirmation and return/resume.

Record date, headset/browser version, seated/standing settings, results, failures and screenshots from the actual headset. Human difficulty, encounter pacing and long-session comfort remain open tuning gates.
