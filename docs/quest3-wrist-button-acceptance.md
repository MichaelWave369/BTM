# Quest 3 / Quest 3S: press-to-open wrist Fieldlink

This is a candidate for **physical headset acceptance**, not a certification from simulated CI.

1. Walk forward in Immersive VR. Raise and turn the left wrist while holding movement. **Do not press the watch.** The Fieldlink menu must stay closed, and walking must continue uninterrupted.
2. Face the little watch toward you. Point the right Touch controller at the distinct OPEN MENU button occupying its lower-right area, then click with RIGHT TRIGGER. Only then may the large panel open.
3. Verify the large screen is directly ABOVE the wrist (less than 0.3 meters center-to-center), with NO two tether lines. It must rotate and translate WITH the watch as you move the left controller, and every button must be reachable by right-controller ray in BOTH eyes.
4. With the panel open, keep the left hand still and turn/look around with the headset, including away from the panel. The panel must stay at the SAME wrist-relative position and orientation without chasing your gaze or disappearing. A user-opened Fieldlink should pause combat simulation but leave the menu functional. Test weapons, map, pause, Ori, and close.
5. While holding the left movement stick forward, press CLOSE MENU and release the right trigger. Walking must resume without recentering the movement stick; shooting and abilities remain blocked until their inputs are released.
6. Lower/turn the LEFT wrist away (not merely your head); the menu must close itself after the short guard, and there should be no invisible input blocker.
7. Left grip is still Ori, including at raised height. Confirm it cannot summon Fieldlink.
8. Test seated/standing, both glove appearances, recenter, tracking interruptions, nearby walls, repeated XR entry, old saved preferences and HUD-off behavior.
9. Recheck Classic, Xbox, VR Arcade, campaign/Pit saves, offline, and multiplayer separately.

The small watch status surface itself remains independent of the large panel. The **initial click** requires a visible, face-up watch. Once opened, the panel shares the watch's local transform and stays active without headset-gaze ownership until the wrist is lowered/turned away, manually closed, or tracking is lost. Old gaze/raised/manual settings sanitize to button mode, preventing a prior preference from re-enabling unwanted automatic activation.\n\n**Build sanity:** A screen showing **UNPIN** and two long cyan tethers is a pre-button-era build (or recording). The newer button build says **CLOSE MENU** and has no tethers. If those old elements appear, exit and reopen the Quest browser tab and reload the live GitHub Pages site before evaluating this rung.
