# Quest 3 / Quest 3S: press-to-open wrist Fieldlink

This is a candidate for **physical headset acceptance**, not a certification from simulated CI.

1. Walk forward in Immersive VR. Raise and turn the left wrist while holding movement. **Do not press the watch.** The Fieldlink menu must stay closed, and walking must continue uninterrupted.
2. Face the little watch toward you. Point the right Touch controller at the distinct OPEN MENU button occupying its lower-right area, then click with RIGHT TRIGGER. Only then may the large panel open.
3. Verify the large screen is directly ABOVE the wrist (less than 0.3 meters center-to-center), with NO two tether lines. It must rotate and translate WITH the watch as you move the left controller, and every button must be reachable by right-controller ray in BOTH eyes.
4. With the panel open, keep the left hand still and look around with the headset. The panel must stay at the same wrist-relative position and orientation. Keep walking while selecting weapons, map, and Ori. Confirm the world keeps advancing and a missed pointer ray cannot fire a shot or turn the player. Use PAUSE or the right-stick click to pause solo play.
5. Hold the left stick forward from before the opening click through CLOSE MENU. Walking should continue throughout. Keep the right trigger down and move the pointer away. The closing press must never fire. Release the trigger and press again to fire. Repeat with a held left-trigger charge and verify its release is consumed before the next charge can fire.
6. Lower/turn the LEFT wrist away (not merely your head); the menu must close itself after the short guard, and there should be no invisible input blocker.
7. Left grip is still Ori, including at raised height. Confirm it cannot summon Fieldlink.
8. Test seated/standing, both glove appearances, recenter, tracking interruptions, nearby walls, repeated XR entry, old saved preferences and HUD-off behavior.
9. Recheck Classic, Xbox, VR Arcade, campaign/Pit saves, offline, and multiplayer separately.

The small watch status surface remains independent of the large panel. The initial click requires a visible, face-up watch. The open panel shares the watch's local transform until the wrist is lowered or turned away, the player closes it, or tracking is lost. Old gaze, raised and manual settings migrate to button mode.

The button-era build shows **CLOSE MENU** and has no tethers. If you see **UNPIN** and two long cyan lines, reload the intended build and check its revision before recording results.
