# Quest 3 / Quest 3S: press-to-open wrist Fieldlink

This is a candidate for **physical headset acceptance**, not a certification from simulated CI.

1. Walk forward in Immersive VR. Raise and turn the left wrist while holding movement. **Do not press the watch.** The Fieldlink menu must stay closed, and walking must continue uninterrupted.
2. Face the little watch toward you. Point the right Touch controller at the distinct OPEN MENU button occupying its lower-right area, then click with RIGHT TRIGGER. Only then may the large panel open.
3. Verify the large screen is directly ABOVE the wrist (less than 0.3 meters center-to-center), no two tether lines, and every control is reachable by right-controller ray in BOTH eyes without straining the arms.
4. A user-opened Fieldlink should pause combat simulation but leave the menu functional. Test weapons, map, pause, Ori, and close.
5. While holding the left movement stick forward, press CLOSE MENU and release the right trigger. Walking must resume without recentering the movement stick; shooting and abilities remain blocked until their inputs are released.
6. Lower/turn the left wrist away; the menu must close itself after the short guard, and there should be no invisible input blocker.
7. Left grip is still Ori, including at raised height. Confirm it cannot summon Fieldlink.
8. Test seated/standing, both glove appearances, recenter, tracking interruptions, nearby walls, repeated XR entry, old saved preferences and HUD-off behavior.
9. Recheck Classic, Xbox, VR Arcade, campaign/Pit saves, offline, and multiplayer separately.

The small watch status surface itself remains independent of the large panel. Old gaze/raised/manual settings now sanitize to button mode, preventing a prior preference from re-enabling unwanted automatic activation.
